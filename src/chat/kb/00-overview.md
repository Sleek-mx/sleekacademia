# Sleek Academia — what it is

pages: /, /about.html

Sleek Academia is a personal academic support studio for serious students and working
professionals. Tagline: "Pass. Guaranteed."

Four fields are supported:

- **Nursing and healthcare** — coursework, clinical reasoning, NCLEX-RN preparation, pathophysiology,
  pharmacology, medical-surgical, mental health, maternal and child health, community health,
  nursing leadership, evidence-based practice.
- **Law and professional exams** — UBE / bar exam planning, constitutional law, contracts, criminal
  law and procedure, civil procedure, evidence, torts, property, legal research and writing.
- **Technology (ICT)** — CompTIA Security+, Network+, A+, cybersecurity, cloud, networking,
  programming, databases, systems analysis.
- **Finance and accounting** — CFA Level I, financial and managerial accounting, corporate finance,
  investments, economics, quantitative methods, taxation.

Four kinds of support:

1. **Essay, report and coursework support** — writing, research, presentations, structured feedback.
2. **Exam preparation** — a study plan, focused review, or support for a professional exam.
3. **One-to-one tutoring** — ongoing guidance on a course, subject, or difficult concept.
4. **Something else** — any custom academic or professional learning need.

How the work runs: the student shares the brief, the team clarifies the scope, and everything after
that — messages, files, milestones, payments — happens inside a private workspace tied to that one
order. Every completed essay or report includes an **AI-use report** for transparency, and the team
reviews instructions, sources, citations and the finished work before delivery.
