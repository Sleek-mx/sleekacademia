# Store and blog

pages: /store.html, /blog.html, /blogs.html

**Store** — `/store.html`. A shelf of ready-made digital study materials. Checkout is handled by
**Gumroad**, not on this site, and each item is a one-time digital purchase. The live catalogue is
pulled from Gumroad; the core titles are:

- *Ophthalmic Pharmacology Q&A* — 56 questions with rationales on ophthalmic medications and nursing
  implications.
- *Patient Education and Drug Therapy Q&A* — practice questions and rationales on safe teaching,
  medication use and patient understanding.
- *OTC Drugs and Herbal Supplements Q&A* — nonprescription drugs, supplements, interactions and
  patient safety.

Store purchases are separate from personalised services. Personalised work always starts as a
request at `/onboard.html`.

**Blog** — `/blog.html`. Study plans, exam strategies and career guidance for NCLEX, the bar exam,
CFA Level I and CompTIA candidates. `/blogs.html` redirects there. Do not invent article titles;
send people to the blog index.
