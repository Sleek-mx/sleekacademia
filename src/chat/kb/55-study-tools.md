# Free study tools

pages: /resources

A free resource board at `/resources` for online college students, no account needed. Filterable by
category: Planning & Time, Study Methods, Writing & Research, Exams & Confidence, Online Learning.

Six resources: Weekly Study Planner, Assignment Breakdown, Reading Notes Template, Exam Prep Map,
Academic Writing Checklist, Online Class Reset. Each one currently routes into the free readiness
check so the team can send it over personally — they are not yet instant downloads.

If someone asks for a specific resource, point them to `/resources` and mention the team follows up
personally once they start the free check.
