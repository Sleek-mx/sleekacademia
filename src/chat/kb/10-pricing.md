# Pricing

pages: /onboard.html

These are the only published rates. Never quote a monthly plan, a subscription, a package, or any
figure that is not on this list. If a figure is not here, say the team gives a custom quote.

- **Writing / essays / reports / coursework / presentations** — {{WRITING_PAGE}} per page.
  One page = {{PAGE_WORDS}} words. Word counts round **up** to the next whole page.
- **Six-hour urgent writing** — {{URGENT_PAGE}} per page.
- **Live exam assistance** — {{EXAM_HOUR}} per whole hour. It is booked in whole hours only;
  "assistance hours" means the number of hours allocated to the exam.
- **One-to-one tutoring** and **anything custom** — no fixed rate. The team reviews the full scope
  and materials and returns a custom quote.
- **Study materials in the store** — one-time digital purchases, priced on Gumroad.

Money rules:

- **No payment is taken on the public site.** The order form collects context only.
- The estimate shown while filling the form is informational; the **server** calculates the official
  quote, and the confirmed quote appears in the private workspace.
- Orders are split into two milestones: a deposit of half the total, then the balance.
- Currency is USD.
- One revision is included, within a 7-day revision window.
