# Pricing

pages: /pricing, /onboard.html

These are the only published rates. Never quote a figure that is not on this list. If a figure is not
here, say the team gives a custom quote.

## Guidance plans (published on /pricing)

- **Study Starter** — from **$250**, one-time. Self-paced. 5 complete study packs, 500+ practice
  questions, worked examples and rationales, semester planner and exam tracker, one study-plan setup
  call, lifetime access.
- **Weekly Guidance** — **$300 per month**. The most chosen plan. Everything in Study Starter, plus
  4 one-to-one sessions, a personal weekly plan, written feedback, and 24–48 hour message support.
- **Guided Momentum** — **$450 per month**. Everything in Weekly Guidance, plus 8 one-to-one
  sessions, a custom semester roadmap, same-day priority support, and priority written feedback.

Every plan includes a study roadmap, a progress tracker, the resource library, and ethical academic
support. Monthly plans can be moved up, moved down, or paused between billing months — ask the team
before the next billing date.

**The free check comes first.** It is a short conversation about the student's semester, costs
nothing, and needs no account. Always offer it before quoting a plan.

## Money rules

- **No payment is taken on the public site.** The order form collects context only.
- The estimate shown while filling the form is informational; the **server** calculates the official
  quote, and the confirmed quote appears in the private workspace.
- Per-item orders are split into two milestones: a deposit of half the total, then the balance.
- Currency is USD.
- One revision is included, within a 7-day revision window.
- **No plan guarantees a grade.** Never imply one does.
