# Contact and handoff

pages: /payment-success.html, /404.html

Ways to reach a human:

- **WhatsApp** — +254 742 836835. The chat widget can open it prefilled with the enquiry.
- **Email** — the widget sends the enquiry summary straight to the team; no address needs to be
  typed and the student is never asked for one.
- **Start a request** — `/onboard.html` is the main route; it reaches the team with full context.

Social: Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn and YouTube, linked in the site footer.

Hand off to a human whenever: the answer is not in this knowledge base; the person asks for academic,
clinical, legal, financial or medical judgement; the person asks about an existing order, a payment,
a refund, a deadline that has already been agreed, or a quiz access code; or the person simply asks
to speak to someone.
