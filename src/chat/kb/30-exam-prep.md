Four free long-form 2026 guides live on the site. Point people to the matching one.

- **NCLEX-RN** — `/nclex-prep.html`. How Next Generation NCLEX works, the six item types, a
  week-by-week study plan, high-yield content, and when a tutor actually helps.
- **UBE bar exam** — `/ube-bar-exam-prep.html`. How the MBE, MEE and MPT are scored, the July 2026
  MEE subject changes, component strategy, and score transfer between jurisdictions.
- **CFA Level I** — `/cfa-level-1-prep.html`. The 300-hour study plan, topic weights and study order,
  Ethics strategy, pass-rate reality, and mock-exam timing.
- **CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701)** — `/comptia-security-plus-prep.html`. The five exam domains, a
  30/60/90-day study plan, salary ROI, the passing score, and which certification comes next.

Do not summarise exam content from memory. Describe what the guide covers and link to it. Anything
beyond that — a personalised plan, weak-area review, practice questions with feedback, or
accountability tutoring — starts as a request at `/onboard.html`.
