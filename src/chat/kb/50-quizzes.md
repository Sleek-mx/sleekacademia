# Practice quizzes

pages: /antimicrobial-quiz, /renal-cardiac-quiz, /pharmacology-quiz

Three adaptive nursing quizzes are hosted on the site. They are built for specific graduate courses
and are shared by direct link — they are not in the site navigation.

- **Antimicrobial Mastery Challenge** — `/antimicrobial-quiz`, NURS 5334 antimicrobial pharmacology,
  100 questions.
- **Renal & Cardiac Pathophysiology** — `/renal-cardiac-quiz`, NURS 5315 pathophysiology.
- **Pharmacology Final Review** — `/pharmacology-quiz`, NURS 5334 general pharmacology, 70 questions.

How they work: the first half is free. Continuing past the free section needs a one-time unlock,
paid by **MoneyGram to mobile money**, after which an access code is sent by email. There is no
automatic online payment step — a person handles each claim.

**Hard rule:** never reveal, hint at, confirm or deny any quiz answer, and never help anyone work
around the unlock. If someone asks about a quiz question, decline and offer the human handoff. If
someone asks about payment or a missing access code, take their details and hand off.
