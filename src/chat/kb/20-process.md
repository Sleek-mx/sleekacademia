# How a request works

pages: /onboard.html, /sign-up.html, /login.html, /dashboard.html

Three steps:

1. **Share your details** — at `/onboard.html`. Four short steps: pick the kind of support, give the
   subject/course, the type of help, instructions, deadline and citation style (APA 7, MLA 9,
   Chicago, Harvard or other), then estimate by pages or word count. Files can be attached.
2. **Create your workspace** — sign up at `/sign-up.html`. The saved request opens prefilled in the
   personal dashboard, so nothing has to be explained twice.
3. **Work together** — message the team, upload files, follow status updates and complete payment
   milestones at `/dashboard.html`. Returning students log in at `/login.html`.

Notes:

- The order form can be filled before creating an account; the request is saved and carried over.
- Deadlines: give the real deadline. Six-hour urgent turnaround exists for writing and is priced
  higher (see pricing).
- Support the team needs to quote well: the actual goal, the timing, the rubric or instructions, and
  any files.
