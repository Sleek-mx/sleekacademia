Everything you need to know.

Can I start with the free check?

Yes. The free check is a short conversation about your semester — your deadlines, the classes giving you trouble, and how much time you actually have. Nothing is charged, and you do not need an account to start one.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Monthly plans can be moved up or down, or paused, between billing months — semesters get heavier and lighter, and the plan should follow. Message us before your next billing date and we will make the change.

Are study tools included?

The free guides, planners and checklists on the study tools page are open to everyone, with no account. Study Starter adds the full study packs and question banks, and both monthly plans include everything in Study Starter.

Does coaching guarantee a grade?

No, and anyone promising otherwise is not being straight with you. We help you plan the week, understand difficult material, and prepare properly — the work, and the grade, stay yours. We do not sit exams, write assessed work, or complete coursework on your behalf.
