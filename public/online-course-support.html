Common questions

Before you ask.

What does online course support include?

Weekly deliverables across the whole course: discussion posts and replies, weekly assignments, quizzes and exams, papers and projects, and participation requirements, all handled against your syllabus schedule.

Is this confidential?

Yes. Course access, personal details, and order history stay inside your private workspace. Nothing is shared, and completed work is never reused.

How do I track progress?

Your private dashboard shows each weekly deliverable, its status, and what is coming next. You receive weekly updates and can message the team at any point.

What platforms do you support?

Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle, Brightspace, and D2L are supported as standard. Other learning platforms can be accommodated after a quick access review.

How is pricing calculated?

Pricing is custom and weekly, based on the course load: the number of weekly deliverables, the subject level, and the weeks remaining. You receive a free quote before anything is confirmed.
